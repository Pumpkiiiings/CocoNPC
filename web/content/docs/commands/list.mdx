---
title: /coconpc list
description: List every loaded NPC with its id and coordinates.
---

Prints every NPC currently in the registry, with its numeric id and position.

---

## Syntax

```bash
/coconpc list
```

No arguments.

---

## Output

```text
[CocoNPC] --- Spawned NPCs ---
ID: 1 - Location: 145.2, 68.0, -210.5
ID: 2 - Location: -34.8, 71.0, 102.1
ID: 3 - Location: 0.5, 120.0, 0.5
```

If nothing is loaded you get `There are no NPCs spawned.` instead.

The exact formatting comes from `NPC_LIST_HEADER` and `NPC_LIST_ENTRY` in `messages.yml`,
so you can change it.

---

## Why you need it

NPCs are packet-only and invisible to command selectors, so there is no vanilla way to find
one. This is how you locate them.

The listing is read straight from memory, so it costs nothing and never loads chunks.

Pair it with [`/coconpc tp <id>`](/docs/commands/teleport) to jump to anything you find,
and use it after a restart to confirm every NPC came back.

<Callout type="info">
  The list shows coordinates but not world names. An NPC in another dimension still appears
  — `/coconpc tp` will take you across worlds.
</Callout>
