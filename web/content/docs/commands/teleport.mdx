---
title: /coconpc tp & tphere
description: Teleport yourself to an NPC, or move an NPC to you.
---

Two directions of the same idea.

---

## Go to an NPC

```bash
/coconpc tp <id>
```

*Alias: `/coconpc teleport <id>`* — permission `CocoNPC.tp`

Moves **you** to the NPC's exact position, across worlds if needed.

```bash
/coconpc tp 12
```

Pair it with [`/coconpc list`](/docs/commands/list) to find the id first.

---

## Bring an NPC to you

```bash
/coconpc tphere <id>
```

Permission `CocoNPC.tphere`

Moves the **NPC** to where you are standing, turned to face you — the same orientation
logic `/coconpc create` uses, so a relocated NPC looks the way a freshly spawned one would.

```bash
/coconpc tphere 12
```

---

## What moves with it

Everything: all eleven body parts, the interaction hitbox, and the hologram. Pose,
rotations and custom offsets are preserved — the NPC arrives exactly as it was, just
somewhere else.

The new position is saved immediately, so it survives a restart.

<Callout type="info">
  Teleporting recalculates who can see the NPC right away, so it appears for players at the
  destination and disappears for players at the origin without waiting for them to move.
</Callout>

---

## Fine positioning

`tphere` gets an NPC to roughly the right place. For placing one precisely — on a chair,
against a wall, on a ledge — use the **Global** button in the
[3D editor](/docs/editor), where modes 5 and 6 move the whole NPC in fractions of a block.
